# DTC

## Description

<span class="red bold">The Ultimate Cast Iron Trio</span>

<span class="red">Product Design / Cookware / Design for Manufacture</span>

<span class="body">The Ultimate Cast Iron Trio explores a cookware system comprising stackable frying pans and a pot with a detachable, interchangeable handle. Designed to combine durability, cooking performance and efficient storage, the collection features a secure handle mechanism that allows the cookware to transition seamlessly between cooking, serving and storage. The handle incorporates a cast iron core and is available in four colours, introducing customisation to a cohesive product system.</span>

## Project Title

RETHINKING COOKWARE & STORAGE

## Introduction

<span class="body">The project explores how thoughtful product design can improve the functionality and storage of everyday cookware. By combining stackable cast iron pots and pans with a detachable handle, the system reduces the space required for storage while maintaining the durability and heat retention associated with cast iron.

The interchangeable handle introduces flexibility across the collection, allowing a single component to serve multiple pieces of cookware. This approach creates a cohesive system focused on practical use, efficient storage and a consistent product language.</span>

## Development

STACKABLE COOKWARE & INTERCHANGEABLE HANDLE

<span class="body">The cookware was developed around a stackable configuration, allowing the pans and pot to nest together when not in use. This arrangement prioritises compact storage while maintaining the robust character of cast iron cookware.

The detachable handle is the central feature of the system. Its attachment mechanism is designed to provide a secure connection during use while allowing the handle to be removed for serving and storage. A cast iron core reinforces the handle's material consistency, while four colour options introduce a degree of personalisation across the collection.</span>

## Process

PRODUCT SYSTEM & FUNCTIONAL INTEGRATION

<span class="body">The design focuses on integrating multiple functional requirements into a unified cookware system. Stackability informs the relationship between the individual vessels, while the interchangeable handle creates a shared interface across the collection.

The project brings together material selection, component integration and storage efficiency to explore a more adaptable approach to cookware design. The resulting concept balances the durability and performance of cast iron with the practical benefits of modularity and reduced storage requirements.</span>
